How to read these grades

These results are based on inspection-level records with an A, B, or C grade. The source dataset is recorded at the violation level, so multiple violation rows from one inspection are grouped together before grade histories are calculated.

Stable AAt least two graded inspections, and every graded inspection in the available history was an A.
Stable BAt least two graded inspections, and every graded inspection in the available history was a B.
Stable CAt least two graded inspections, and every graded inspection in the available history was a C.

“Stable” describes consistency across the records available in this dataset. It does not mean the restaurant was inspected continuously or that its grade never changed outside the recorded period.
